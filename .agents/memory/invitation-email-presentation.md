---
name: Invitation email presentation
description: Visual and content direction for provider-profile invitation emails.
---

Provider-profile invitations use a dark, warm-gold EquiConnected launch aesthetic: centered brand treatment, welcoming headline, concise profile-completion copy, one clear call to action, expiry notice, and a compact launch-location footer.

**Why:** The preferred reference establishes a premium launch identity, but these emails remain transactional and must clearly explain the profile invitation and secure action.

**How to apply:** Use table-based, inline-styled HTML for broad email-client support and retain a complete plain-text alternative. Keep any email CTA tied to the secure invitation URL; do not add non-functional marketing controls such as an unsubscribe link to this transactional message.
