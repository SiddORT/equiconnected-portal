---
name: Public provider map boundary
description: Product authorization and privacy boundary for anonymous provider discovery.
---

Anonymous homepage discovery may include providers only when their listing is both active and published and has a coordinate-bearing location. Publication is the product-approved consent boundary for appearing on the public care map.

**Why:** The public homepage needs real provider markers without weakening the verified-member directory. Public discovery was explicitly approved for published listings, while private contact and precise street-address fields were not needed for the experience.

**How to apply:** Public map responses may expose provider name, type, specialization names, ratings, review count, city/region/country, coordinates, and a selected public photo. Do not expose email, phone, full street address, reviews, or unpublished/inactive listings. Keep richer directory access behind member verification.
