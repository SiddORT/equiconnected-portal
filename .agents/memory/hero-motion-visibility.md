---
name: Hero motion visibility
description: Why homepage hero animation needs perceptible movement and mobile-safe framing.
---

Treat homepage hero motion as a visible live effect within a few seconds, not merely an animation that exists in code. Keep the motion calm and honor reduced-motion preferences.

**Why:** A slow opacity-only glow and brief text entrance looked static to the user despite technically animating.

**How to apply:** Compare live states several seconds apart. Check narrow mobile framing at the furthest zoom as well as the reduced-motion presentation before calling a hero-motion change done.
