---
name: Homepage photo permission boundary
description: Credits require image-specific evidence; prototype credits can misidentify authors.
---

Do not remove a homepage photo credit based only on local hosting, a CDN URL,
or the prototype's photographer name. Establish an image-specific licence or
rights-holder permission; otherwise replace only when replacement is authorized.

**Why:** The supplied prototype contained at least one incorrect photographer
name, and the owner portrait's original CDN URL did not establish its licence.
The user initially required unresolved credits to remain, then explicitly
authorized replacing the remaining owner portrait if its licence could not be
established. This authorization is specific to that portrait, not other images.

**How to apply:** Consult the internal [photo permission record](../../docs/homepage-photo-licences.md)
for evidence and unresolved exceptions, but recheck it against the actual image
before relying on a previous decision. A standard photo licence, a paid
Unsplash+ entitlement and API attribution obligations are different questions;
none establishes model/property releases.
