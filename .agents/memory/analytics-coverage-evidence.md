---
name: Analytics coverage evidence
description: Distinguish retained event dates from independently established collection coverage.
---

Do not treat the earliest retained business record as the start of complete
historical reporting coverage. It proves that one event was retained, not that
earlier days had no events or that records were never removed.

**Why:** Account deletion and development resets can remove transactional data;
inventing a coverage boundary from surviving records makes previous-period
comparisons appear more certain than the available evidence supports.

**How to apply:** Report first/last retained event dates as such. Require an
independently recorded collection boundary before claiming complete coverage
for historical comparisons. Prospective traffic has an explicit rollout
boundary; keep its unavailable pre-rollout intervals distinct from genuine
covered zeroes, and keep legacy homepage aggregates separate.
