## Explicit scope findings

- The frontend ships provider/member role-specific account screens and a public contact enquiry form. The contact form sends a message to the EquiConnected team and says a reply will come by email; it is not provider messaging.
- Private member/provider messaging is a separate, shipped workflow from the public contact form and private platform feedback. Verified members can start a conversation only when a listing is active and published with one eligible, explicitly linked provider account; providers can only open and reply to member-started conversations. The member Messages action is conditional on availability, while published Phone, Email, and Website links remain direct-contact alternatives.
- Messaging contracts are implemented under `/api/v1/messages`: availability and conversation start, paginated inbox and unread count, participant-scoped conversation/thread retrieval with `before_sequence` paging, replies, and read receipts. The backend requires verified member/provider participants, checks the explicit provider-account/listing link, requires member contact-sharing consent, and encrypts message and shared-contact content at rest. Content is protected in transit but is not end-to-end encrypted. Email notifications are best-effort and omit conversation text; notification failure does not mean an accepted message was not saved and does not guarantee delivery, a response, or an appointment.
- Opening and foregrounding the messaging page refreshes inbox/thread data, with visible-page polling; received messages are marked read when rendered messages become visible. Inbox pages contain up to 20 conversations and thread history loads in batches of 50. This is a private correspondence feature, not appointment booking or emergency dispatch.
- Provider Insights is available from the provider portal navigation at `/provider/insights` and is scoped server-side to the single explicitly linked provider listing for the authenticated provider account. `GET /api/v1/provider/portal/insights` accepts `preset`, `date_from`, and `date_to` only; the custom dates require `preset=custom`, and callers cannot choose a `provider_id`. A missing/ambiguous listing link returns a provider-portal access error. The report is aggregate-only. The audited page/API do not suppress low counts, so small totals may be visible; privacy comes from role/listing authorization and aggregate reporting, not a minimum-count threshold.
- Insights activity periods use inclusive calendar dates in the configured system timezone, converted to UTC timestamp bounds. Presets cover the last 7 days, last 30 days, and current month; custom ranges are limited to 366 inclusive days and cannot include future dates. The Current snapshot (saved members, ratings, visible written reviews, average rating) is independent of that selected range. Source coverage distinguishes full, partial, unavailable, and unknown; a covered zero is not the same as missing/unavailable coverage. The page has a manual Refresh control, metric definitions, trend charts with View data table controls, contact-link breakdown, and no export control.
- No member self-service password-reset form appears on `/login`. The provider recovery screen consumes administrator-issued reset links; the provider login page has no self-service recovery control.
- Provider profile approval and provider application review are administrator-controlled. The provider-facing portal shows review notices and provides no publish or operational controls. This checklist documents only what providers and members can do.
- Public homepage copy specifically says provider profiles/contact information are members-only, recorded visits are not appointments, and EquiConnected is not an emergency dispatch service; those limits are reflected in the related topics.
