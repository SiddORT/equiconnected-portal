### Animation concept: Pony transforms into a realistic horse

Build a cinematic 3D browser animation using **Three.js/WebGL**.

The scene begins with a **small young pony** walking slowly through shallow water. The pony should have recognizable horse anatomy: four legs with correct joints and hooves, a proper horse head and muzzle, ears, neck, torso, mane, and tail.

As the pony walks forward through the water, magical water begins swirling around its legs and body.

The pony then gradually transforms into a **FULL-GROWN, REALISTIC ADULT HORSE**.

### VERY IMPORTANT — final horse quality

The final animal MUST clearly look like a proper real horse.

Do NOT make it look like:

* a fantasy monster
* a deer
* a dog
* a generic quadruped
* a cartoon horse
* an oversized pony

The adult horse must have:

* realistic horse proportions
* long, muscular legs
* clearly defined hooves
* large horse torso/chest
* long natural neck
* anatomically recognizable horse head
* proper muzzle and nostrils
* two horse ears
* realistic mane
* realistic flowing tail
* natural horse posture and movement
* believable muscle structure
* realistic galloping/walking motion

The transformation should make the pony visibly **grow taller and more muscular**, with its legs lengthening, chest and torso becoming larger, neck extending, and mane/tail becoming fuller.

### Water effect

The horse is walking through shallow realistic water.

Include:

* water surface
* ripples around the hooves
* small splashes when the hooves touch the water
* water droplets
* waves spreading outward
* swirling water wrapping around the pony during transformation

During the transformation, the water should temporarily cover parts of the pony and then flow away to reveal the adult horse.

### Animation sequence

1. Start with a cinematic view of the small pony.
2. Pony walks naturally through shallow water.
3. Camera slowly follows the pony.
4. Water begins swirling around its legs.
5. The swirling water rises around its body.
6. The pony begins growing and transforming.
7. Its legs become longer and more muscular.
8. Its torso, neck, head, mane, and tail transform into adult horse proportions.
9. Water bursts outward and reveals the completed adult horse.
10. The adult horse pauses for a moment.
11. It then performs a natural horse gallop through the water.
12. Finish with a cinematic camera pull-back as the horse runs away.

### Visual style

Make it cinematic and realistic rather than cartoon-like.

Use:

* realistic lighting
* natural shadows
* reflections on the water
* atmospheric environment
* subtle fog/mist
* realistic water movement
* smooth animation
* cinematic camera movement

The final shot should feel like a scene from a high-quality 3D animated film.

### Technical requirements

* Use Three.js/WebGL.
* Keep the animation performant in a normal desktop browser.
* Make `/animation` responsive.
* Add a **Play / Replay** button.
* Add a loading state while 3D assets are loading.
* Keep the animation isolated to `/animation`.
* Do not remove or alter existing application functionality.
* If a high-quality horse 3D model is required, structure the code so a GLTF/GLB horse model can easily be replaced or added later.
* Prioritize the **quality and recognizability of the adult horse** over unnecessary visual effects.

Before finishing, test `/animation` and make sure the final transformation produces a clearly recognizable **realistic adult horse**.
